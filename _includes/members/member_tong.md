**Prof. Zhiguo Tong** (童志国) — Principal Investigator

Tenure-track professor and doctoral supervisor, School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering, Beihang University; National-level Young Talent awardee. His research focuses on power management integrated circuit design, including data center power delivery and AI-assisted power chip design. He has published or had accepted 30+ academic papers, including 7 as first author at top-tier IC design conferences and journals: ISSCC, JSSC, and the VLSI Symposium.
