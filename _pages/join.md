The BUAA iPES Lab (北航集成功率电子与系统实验室) is recruiting graduate students (M.Sc./Ph.D.) interested in **power management integrated circuits and systems**, including topics such as high-efficiency/high-density power delivery, wireless power transfer (WPT), and power IC design for robotics and computing applications.

### What we look for

- Solid background in analog/mixed-signal IC design, power electronics, or a closely related field
- Strong interest in hands-on chip design, tape-out, and measurement
- Self-motivated and willing to work as part of a small, growing team

### How to apply

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send your CV and a brief statement of research interests to [zgtong@buaa.edu.cn](mailto:zgtong@buaa.edu.cn).
