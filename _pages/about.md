The **Integrated Power Electronics and Systems (iPES) Lab** (北航集成功率电子与系统实验室) is a research group at Beihang University (BUAA) led by Dr. Zhiguo Tong. Our research centers on **power management integrated circuits and systems** (电源管理集成电路与系统), including high-efficiency, high-density power delivery architectures and circuits for applications such as humanoid robots, high-performance computing, and wireless power transfer.

We are always looking for motivated graduate students to join us — see the [Join Us](/join-us/) page for details.

#### About the PI

Dr. Zhiguo Tong received his Ph.D. degree from the Laboratory of Analog and Mixed-Signal VLSI, University of Macau, in 2024, his M.Sc. degree from Fudan University in 2021, and his B.E. degree from Tianjin University in 2018. In the summer of 2024, he was a visiting scholar with the IMS-MX Group, Leibniz University Hannover, Germany. He was awarded the IEEE SSCS Pre-doctoral Achievement 2024–2025, the Best Student Poster Award at PwrSoC 2023, and the Best Oral Presentation Award of the THU-UM Sustainable Innovation Workshop 2023, among others.
