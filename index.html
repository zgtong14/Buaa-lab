北航集成功率电子与系统实验室 — Beihang Integrated Power Electronics and Systems (BiPES) Lab

Data center power delivery — hybrid DC-DC converters from rack to chip 数据中心供电技术 —— 从机架到芯片的混合型 DC-DC 变换器
Robot joint motor drives — high-voltage power delivery for humanoid robots 机器人关节电机驱动 —— 面向人形机器人的高压电源传输
Radiation-hardened power circuits for satellite and aerospace platforms 抗辐照电源电路 —— 面向卫星及航空航天平台
AI for Power — machine-learning-assisted power IC design AI for Power —— 机器学习辅助的电源芯片设计

The BiPES Lab (Beihang Integrated Power Electronics and Systems Lab) is established at the School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering, Beihang University (BUAA), led by Prof. Zhiguo Tong. We work on power management integrated circuits and systems, delivering high-performance power IC solutions for applications — data center computing, robotic electromechanical systems, and space/extreme-radiation environments — that demand the utmost in power conversion efficiency, power density, and reliability. We also explore AI-assisted power chip design (AI for Power), advancing the convergence of power electronics with intelligent design methods.

北航集成功率电子与系统实验室（BiPES Lab） 依托北京航空航天大学集成电路科学与工程学院组建。实验室专注于 电源管理集成电路与系统 研究，致力于为数据中心算力、机器人机电系统、空间及极端辐照环境等对电能变换效率、功率密度与可靠性有极致要求的场景，提供高性能电源芯片解决方案；同时探索以人工智能方法赋能电源芯片设计（AI for Power），推动电源电子技术与智能化设计方法的深度融合。

Join Us加入我们

The BUAA BiPES Lab is recruiting graduate students (M.Sc./Ph.D.) interested in power management integrated circuits and systems, including topics such as high-efficiency/high-density power delivery, wireless power transfer (WPT), and power IC design for robotics and computing applications.

Who we recruit

  • Ph.D./M.Sc. students with a strong academic record and a background in integrated circuit design and research experience (tape-out experience preferred)
  • Undergraduates — research internships and undergraduate thesis projects are open year-round
  • Postdocs — applications accepted year-round; candidates with a background in power electronics research are especially welcome

What we look for

  • Solid background in analog/mixed-signal IC design, power electronics, or a closely related field
  • Strong interest in hands-on chip design, tape-out, and measurement
  • Self-motivated and willing to work as part of a small, growing team

How to apply

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send your CV and a brief statement of research interests to zgtong@buaa.edu.cn.
