Prof. Zhiguo Tong (童志国) — Principal Investigator

Tenure-track professor and doctoral supervisor, School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering, Beihang University; National-level Young Talent awardee. His research focuses on power management integrated circuit design, including data center power delivery and AI-assisted power chip design. He has published or had accepted 30+ academic papers, including 7 as first author at top-tier IC design conferences and journals: ISSCC, JSSC, and the VLSI Symposium.

Education and Experience

Tianjin University, B.E.; Fudan University, M.Sc., advised by Prof. Zhiliang Hong; University of Macau, Ph.D., advised by Prof. Yan Lu; Tsinghua University, Visiting Ph.D. Student; University of Macau, Assistant Researcher; University of Pennsylvania, Postdoctoral Researcher

Honors

IEEE SSCS Pre-Doctoral Achievement Award; APEC 2026 Best Presentation Award; PwrSoC 2023 Best Student Poster Award

童志国 — 课题组负责人

北京航空航天大学集成电路科学与工程学院准聘教授，博士生导师，国家级青年人才。研究方向为电源管理集成电路设计，围绕数据中心供电技术、AI 辅助电源芯片设计等前沿领域开展研究。累计发表/录用学术论文 30 余篇，以第一作者身份在集成电路设计领域顶级会议及期刊 ISSCC、JSSC、VLSI Symposium 发表论文 7 篇。

教育及工作经历
