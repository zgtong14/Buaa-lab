Prof. Zhiguo Tong (童志国) — Principal Investigator

Professor and doctoral supervisor, School of Integrated Circuit Science and Engineering, Beihang University; National-level Young Talent awardee. His research focuses on power management integrated circuit design, spanning data center power delivery, robot joint motor drive circuits, radiation-hardened power circuits, and AI-assisted power chip design. He has published or had accepted 30+ academic papers, including 7 as first or co-first author at top-tier IC design venues — ISSCC, JSSC, and the VLSI Symposium.

Education

Ph.D., University of Macau (2021–2025), advised by Prof. Yan Lu · M.Sc., Fudan University (2018–2021), advised by Prof. Zhiliang Hong · B.E., Tianjin University (2014–2018)

Experience

Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Pennsylvania (2025.10–2026.5) · Assistant Researcher, University of Macau (2025.2–2025.8) · Visiting Ph.D. Student, Tsinghua University (2024.9–2025.1)

Honors

IEEE SSCS Pre-Doctoral Achievement Award · APEC 2026 Best Presentation Award · ISSCC 2023 Highlighted Paper

童志国 教授 (Prof. Zhiguo Tong) — 负责人

北京航空航天大学集成电路科学与工程学院教授，博士生导师，国家级青年人才。研究方向为电源管理集成电路设计，围绕数据中心供电技术、机器人关节电机驱动电路、抗辐照电源电路、AI 辅助电源芯片设计等前沿领域开展研究。累计发表/录用学术论文 30 余篇，以第一/共同第一作者身份在集成电路设计领域顶级会议及期刊 ISSCC、JSSC、VLSI Symposium 发表论文 7 篇。

教育经历
