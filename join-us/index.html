graduate student and collaboration openings at the BUAA IPES Lab

The BUAA IPES Lab is recruiting graduate students (M.Sc./Ph.D.) interested in power management integrated circuits and systems, including topics such as high-efficiency/high-density power delivery, wireless power transfer (WPT), and power IC design for robotics and computing applications.

### Who We Recruit

  • Ph.D./M.Sc. students with a strong academic record and a background in integrated circuit design and research experience (tape-out experience preferred) - Undergraduates — research internships and undergraduate thesis projects are open year-round - Postdocs — applications accepted year-round; candidates with a background in power electronics research are especially welcome

### What We Look For

  • Solid background in analog/mixed-signal IC design, power electronics, or a closely related field - Strong interest in hands-on chip design, tape-out, and measurement - Self-motivated and willing to work as part of a small, growing team

### How to Apply

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send your CV and a brief statement of research interests to zgtong@buaa.edu.cn.

北航集成功率电子与系统实验室（IPES Lab）正在招募对 电源管理集成电路与系统 感兴趣的硕士/博士研究生，研究方向包括高效率/高密度电源变换、无线电能传输（WPT），以及面向机器人和计算应用的电源芯片设计。

### 招募对象

  • 课题组招收品学兼优，有集成电路设计背景和科研经验的博士/硕士（有流片经验者优先） - 面向本科生开放科研实习及本科毕业设计 - 博士后全年接受申请（欢迎有电力电子研究基础的同学申请）

### 我们希望你具备

  • 扎实的模拟/混合信号集成电路设计、电力电子或相关领域基础 - 对芯片设计、流片和测试有浓厚兴趣，愿意动手实践 - 自我驱动，能够融入一个小而不断成长的团队

### 如何申请

如果你有意加入课题组，请将个人简历和一份简短的研究兴趣陈述发送至 zgtong@buaa.edu.cn。
